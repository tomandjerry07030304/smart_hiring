# Initial Concept
The Smart Hiring System is an AI-powered recruitment platform designed to streamline the hiring process. It automates resume parsing, employs semantic matching for candidate ranking, and integrates fairness evaluations to ensure unbiased recruitment decisions.

# Product Definition

## Target Users
- **HR Professionals and Recruiters:** Seeking to automate candidate screening and manage large volumes of applications efficiently.
- **Hiring Managers:** Requiring objective, AI-driven insights and ranking to make data-supported hiring decisions.
- **Job Applicants:** Looking for a transparent application process, status tracking, and constructive feedback.

## Core Goals
- **Bias Reduction:** Implement AI-driven fairness evaluations (e.g., Fairlearn) to identify and mitigate bias in recruitment.
- **Efficiency through Automation:** Eliminate manual resume screening by automating parsing (PDF/DOCX) and data extraction.
- **Quality of Hire:** Utilize advanced semantic matching (Sentence-BERT) to rank candidates accurately against specific job descriptions.

## Key Features
- **Automated Resume Processing:** High-accuracy extraction of candidate details from various file formats.
- **Semantic Candidate Ranking:** AI-powered matching that understands context beyond simple keyword searches.
- **Fairness & Bias Dashboard:** Visual reporting on recruitment fairness to assist in compliance and ethical hiring.
- **Candidate & Recruiter Dashboards:** Dedicated interfaces for managing applications, tracking status, and internal communication.

## Integration & Deployment
- **Social Integration:** LinkedIn API for seamless candidate profile imports.
- **Cloud Deployment:** Designed for scalable deployment on platforms like Railway, Render, or Vercel.
- **Compliance Reporting:** Generation of exportable PDF/Excel reports for HR audits.
- **Communication Engine:** Automated email notification system for real-time updates.
