# Product Guidelines

## Communication Style
- **HR & Recruiters:** The tone should be professional, authoritative, and trustworthy. Use clear and direct language to instill confidence in the system's AI capabilities and data integrity.
- **Candidates:** The tone should be warm, welcoming, and encouraging. Focus on reducing application anxiety and providing supportive feedback throughout the process.
- **Documentation:** Technical documentation must be precise for developers (API specs, ML architecture) while remaining accessible and jargon-free for non-technical stakeholders (project summaries, user guides).

## Visual Identity & UI/UX
- **Clarity & Minimalism:** Prioritize a clean interface that minimizes cognitive load. Use whitespace effectively to highlight critical candidate data and navigation paths.
- **Analytical Depth:** Provide data-rich views using modern charts and graphs for bias analysis and candidate ranking metrics. Ensure these complex visualizations remain intuitive.
- **Accessibility (WCAG 2.1):** The application must be fully navigable via keyboard and compatible with screen readers. Maintain high color contrast and semantic HTML.
- **Responsive Design:** A mobile-first approach is mandatory to ensure job seekers have a seamless experience on any device.
- **Internationalization:** The UI should be designed for easy localization to support multiple languages and regional hiring standards.

## AI & Ethical Principles
- **Transparent Scoring:** When ranking candidates, the system must provide the underlying scores and key features (e.g., specific skills, experience matches) that contributed to the ranking.
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** AI-driven insights are presented as suggestions. All final hiring decisions (shortlisting, rejecting) must be made or confirmed by a human user.
- **Bias Mitigation:**
    - **Transparency:** Flagged bias metrics must be accompanied by plain-language explanations of their significance.
    - **Actionability:** Provide clear, recommended actions for hiring managers when potential bias is detected.
    - **Confidentiality:** Aggregated fairness data must never compromise the privacy of individual candidate records.
